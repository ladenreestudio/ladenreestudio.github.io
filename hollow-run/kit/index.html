Longpress · article
Hollow Run is a one-button rhythm runner by La Denrée Studio. Your little cyan ghost runs along a glowing circuit rail at a tempo that rises level after level (120 to 168 BPM). Hold to become a ghost: you turn translucent and pass straight through the magenta walls. Release to become solid again: only a solid runner can collect the golden coins, and only a solid runner survives the crackling cyan fields. Your ghost gauge lasts 2.5 seconds, so every hold is a decision. Twelve hand-tuned lines, a fresh seeded Daily Line every day, PERFECT timing that doubles your coins and a SURGE super-power that smashes through everything. Short sessions, instant retry: easy to learn, hard to perfect.

Hollow Run est un runner rythmique à un seul bouton, signé La Denrée Studio. Un petit fantôme cyan court sur un rail-circuit lumineux, à un tempo qui monte de niveau en niveau (120 à 168 BPM). Maintenir = fantôme : translucide, il traverse les murs magenta. Relâcher = solide : seul un coureur solide ramasse les pièces dorées et survit aux champs cyan. La jauge fantôme dure 2,5 s : chaque maintien est une décision. Douze lignes réglées à la main, une Daily Line inédite chaque jour, le timing PERFECT qui double les pièces et le super-pouvoir SURGE qui fracasse tout.
