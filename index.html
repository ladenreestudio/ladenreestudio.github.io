FIRST GAME

Hold to become a ghost. Release to grab the coins.

Hollow Run is a one-button rhythm runner. Hold to turn into a ghost and pass straight through the magenta walls; release to stay solid, dodge the crackling cyan fields and collect the golden coins.

Twelve hand-tuned levels with a rising tempo, a fresh seeded Daily Line every day, PERFECT timing and the SURGE super-power. Short sessions, instant retry: easy to learn, hard to perfect.

AboutThe studio

La Denrée Studio is a solo indie game studio from France.

We make small one-button arcade games: one input, a clear rule, a rising tempo, and nothing in the way.

Our games run in the browser and on mobile, load fast, work offline and never ask for an account.
